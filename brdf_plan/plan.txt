2. Material Analysis and Classification
Before starting the heavy optimization, the script performs heuristic analysis to categorize the material.

Metallic Region Detection
The function detect_metallic_regions uses an Intensity Ratio Heuristic.

It calculates the ratio between the maximum intensity and median intensity for each pixel across all lighting conditions.

The Logic: Metallic surfaces typically exhibit "dark" behavior in most views but "extremely bright" spikes in specific specular directions. A high max-to-median ratio is a strong indicator of metallic or highly specular behavior.

Morphological Cleanup: It applies Bilateral Filtering to preserve edges and uses Connected Component Labeling (via scipy.ndimage) to remove small "islands" of noise, ensuring a spatially coherent material mask.

Specularity and Roughness Initialization
The analyze_specularity function uses Statistical Signal Variance.

It computes the Coefficient of Variation (CV) (std/mean) of pixel intensities across the light stack.

High variance suggests a "shiny" material with sharp highlights, while low variance suggests a matte material.

The script maps the sharpness of these highlights to an initial Roughness value (α), which serves as the starting point for the GGX optimization.
